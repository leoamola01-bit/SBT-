What happens next?
  1. Our team will review your application within 24-48 hours.
  2. You will be notified of the approval status.
  3. Upon approval, you will be required to pay a one-time administration fee of US $30.00.
  4. After payment confirmation, the vehicle will be delivered to the nearest police station in your area for official paperwork.
  5. Once paperwork is completed at the police station, a company representative accompanied by a police officer will visit your residential address to finalize the handover.
